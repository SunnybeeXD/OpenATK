Basics

Sensitivity
How far the pointer travels when you move the mouse.
Polling rate
How often the mouse reports its position. Higher feels smoother and uses more battery.
Lift-off distance
Maximum working distance from the bottom of the mouse to the surface. Lower suits low-sensitivity players who reposition often.
0.7 mm

Tracking

Movement smoothing
Cleans up your cursor path for steadier aim, at the cost of a tiny bit of latency.
Straight line correction
Forces perfectly straight strokes. Handy for drawing, distracting for games.
Ripple correction
Smooths out wobble during fast flicks.
Long range mode
Stronger wireless signal at a distance. Uses noticeably more battery.
Sensor performance
How hard the sensor samples the surface. The competitive modes scan far more often — lower latency and steadier tracking, shorter battery life. Older firmware may only accept Basic.
Sensor rotation
Turns the sensor’s idea of “straight” to match how you hold the mouse, so a flick you meant to be horizontal lands horizontal.
0°

Lighting

DPI light
The LED that shows which sensitivity stage you are on. Each stage’s colour is set on the Sensitivity card above.

Buttons & power

Scroll wheel anti-mistouch
Ignores the first scroll tick. The wheel only acts if you scroll again within the set time, so a knocked wheel does nothing mid-game.
Click debounce
Ignores repeat clicks for this long. Raise it only if a worn switch starts double-clicking.
Sleep after
Idle time before the mouse naps to save battery. It wakes the moment you move it.
