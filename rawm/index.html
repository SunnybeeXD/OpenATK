Performance

Polling, sensor and tracking behaviour for the current profile.

Polling and sensor
Polling Rate
How many times a second the mouse reports to your PC. Higher cuts delay but uses more battery and CPU; the Performance mode below sets the ceiling.
Performance
Sensor Polling Rate
Glass Mode
Lets the sensor track on glass pads. Polling is held at 1K while it is on.
LOD
Lift-off distance: how high you can lift the mouse before it stops tracking. Lower stops drift when you reposition; too low can drop tracking on soft pads.
Motion processing
Motion Sync
Lines up sensor readings with USB reports so they arrive evenly spaced. Adds a small delay, on average about half a report interval.
Ripple Control
Filters the small jitter sensors show at high DPI. Adds a little delay and can soften very small movements.
Angle Snap
Straightens movements that are close to horizontal or vertical. Good for drawing lines, usually unwanted in games.
Angle Tune
Rotates the sensor by up to 30° either way so straight swipes come out straight with your grip.

Buttons and power
Button Combination
Lets you change settings by holding specific mouse buttons together, without software. Turn it off if you trigger it by accident.
Sleep Time
The mouse sleeps after this long without use, and wakes when you move it.
1 min
Scroll Hysteresis
Ignores the first wheel tick, so a bumped wheel does nothing. The wheel only scrolls if it moves again within the set window. A higher value protects for longer but makes the first scroll feel slower.
