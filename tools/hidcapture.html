HID capture

Records every report the official MelGeek Hive page sends to and gets back from your device, so the protocol can be worked out. Nothing is uploaded: the log stays on your computer until you send the file.

1Install the recorder on the Hive page

  1. Open https://hive.melgeek.com in Chrome or Edge, plug the device in, and let Hive load and show it.
  2. Press F12, open the Console tab, paste the code below and press Enter. If Chrome blocks pasting, type allow pasting, press Enter, then paste again.
  3. A small green panel appears at the bottom right of the Hive page. That is the recorder. Do not refresh the page, a refresh wipes it.
  4. Unplug and replug the device (or switch the dongle off and on), then wait for Hive to reconnect. This captures the start-up handshake, which is the most useful part.
The recorder only listens. It never sends anything to the device itself and never contacts any server. It runs inside Hive's own page, so it sees exactly what Hive sees.

2Do these, one at a time

Before each one, type its name in the green panel and press Mark, then make the change in Hive, then wait two seconds. Marks are what let the packets be matched to a setting. Use values that are easy to tell apart (800, then 1600, then 3200 beats 800 then 850).

Do only what your device has. Skip the rest.

    Do not use firmware update, factory reset, calibration or anything that says it will restart the device. A failed flash can brick it, and none of that is needed for this.

    3Save the log and send it

    Press Download in the green panel. You get a hidcapture-….json file. Open it below to check that it looks right, then send the file.

    The log holds device IDs, firmware or serial strings the device reports, and the bytes of every setting you changed. It does not hold your account, name or anything else from the page.

    +Optional: read the device descriptor

    Lists what the device exposes over HID (vendor and product ID, report sizes). Read-only: it opens nothing and sends nothing.

    This browser has no WebHID. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
