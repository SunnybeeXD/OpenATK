Performance

Polling, sensor and tracking behaviour.

Polling and sensor
Polling Rate
How many times a second the mouse reports to your PC. Higher cuts delay and looks smoother, but uses more battery and CPU. Drop back to 1K if a game stutters.
Lift Off
How high you can lift the mouse before it stops tracking. Lower stops drift when you reposition; too low can drop tracking on soft pads.
Competitive Mode
Switches the sensor to G-Wolves’ competitive tracking mode. G-Wolves doesn’t publish exactly what changes, so try both and keep the one that feels better.
Motion processing
Motion Sync
Lines up sensor readings with USB reports so they arrive evenly spaced. Adds a small delay, on average about half a report interval.
Ripple Control
Filters the small jitter sensors show at high DPI. Adds a little delay and can soften very small movements.
Angle Snap
Straightens movements that are close to horizontal or vertical. Good for drawing lines, usually unwanted in games.
Power
Sleep Time
The mouse sleeps after this long without use, and wakes when you move it.
1 min
