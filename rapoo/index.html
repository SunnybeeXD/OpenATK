Basics

Speed, polling and lift-off. The stuff you feel every second.

Sensitivity
How far the cursor moves for each inch the mouse moves. Higher DPI means less hand movement. This sets the stage the mouse is on now; the DPI button on the mouse switches between stages.
DPI stages and custom sensitivity

Use equal X and Y values for normal sensitivity. Newly enabled stages keep their existing stored DPI.

Polling rate
How many times a second the mouse reports to your PC. Higher rates cut delay and look smoother on fast monitors, but use more battery and more CPU. 4K and 8K need a capable PC; drop back to 1K if a game stutters.
Lift-off distance
How high you can lift the mouse before it stops tracking. Lower stops the cursor drifting when you lift and reposition; too low can drop tracking on soft or uneven pads.
0.7 mm
Onboard profile
The mouse keeps a full set of settings in each profile. Switching loads that profile's settings onto the mouse.

Tracking

How the sensor reads the surface and what it does to your movement.

Sensor
Sensor performance
How often the sensor takes a reading. Higher performance costs more battery. Rapoo stores a separate choice for each polling rate.
Key scan rate
How often the mouse checks its buttons for a click.
Sensor rotation
Rotates the sensor's idea of straight by up to 30° either way. Leave it at zero unless your straight swipes drift up or down.
0°
Motion processing
Times each sensor reading to line up with a USB report, so reports arrive evenly spaced and tracking feels more consistent.
Filters out the tiny back-and-forth jitter sensors show at high DPI. Can soften very small movements.

Buttons & power

Click timing and how the mouse sleeps.

Click debounce
After a click, ignores further clicks for this long. Lower is faster; raise it only if a worn switch starts double-clicking.
More values
Release debounce
How long the mouse filters contact bounce after a button is released.
More values
Wireless
Wireless RF strategy
Adaptive lowers RF power when the signal is strong, which saves battery. Maximum RF keeps full power for better resistance to interference.
Communication protocol
Turbo or Initial wireless protocol. This setting is shared by all profiles.
Power
Sleep after
Idle time before the mouse naps to save battery. It wakes the moment you move it.

Device

Connection details and diagnostics.

Rapoo support
Experimental support for the VT nrf54L family. Physical hardware validation is still needed. Profiles must already exist on the mouse. Remapping, macros, lighting effects, pairing, reset and firmware updates are not included. See the supported models and protocol notes.
