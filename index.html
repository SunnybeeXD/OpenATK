Basics

Sensitivity
How far the pointer travels when you move the mouse.
Polling rate
How often the mouse reports its position. Higher feels smoother and uses more battery.
Lift-off distance
Maximum working distance from the bottom of the mouse to the surface. Lower suits low-sensitivity players who reposition often.

Long range mode

Buttons & power

Click debounce
Ignores repeat clicks for this long. Raise it only if a worn switch starts double-clicking.
Sleep after
Idle time before the mouse naps to save battery. It wakes the moment you move it.
