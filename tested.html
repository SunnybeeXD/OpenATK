Which ATK mice OpenATK is known to work on
Open the app

Two vendor protocols ship under the ATK name and the app speaks both, picking one automatically from the interface the device exposes. Anything on a third interface won't appear in the browser's device picker at all. The name registry covers 251 product IDs, so most ATK and VXE mice should at least connect and identify — the tables below are about what someone has actually put hands on.

Wireless receivers report a generic product name — "Wireless mouse 8k dongle-L1" and the like — so the app reads the CID/MID pair off the link and resolves the mouse that is actually paired. That is why a dongle PID and a mouse PID both appear above.

Reporting a device

Both outcomes are useful, and the second one more so.

If it works — open an issue with the model and PID and it moves into the confirmed table.

If it doesn't — open an issue with the activity log. It's under Device info and troubleshooting in the app, behind Show activity log, and it holds every frame sent and received. That log is the thing that makes a bug report actionable.

If a setting reads back differently to ATK HUB — the log plus a note of what HUB shows is exactly what's needed.
